Privacy

Last updated 18 October 2026

The short version: we do not collect your gift list, we do not use tracking cookies, and we do not run analytics or advertising scripts on this site.

The gift budget calculator

Everything you type into the calculator (your budget, names, amounts and gift ideas) is stored only in your own browser, using its local storage. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. The calculator works offline.

  • Your list stays on the device and browser where you made it. Other people, and your other devices, cannot see it.
  • To delete it, use Clear everything in the calculator, or clear this site's data in your browser settings.
  • Export CSV and Print list create the file or printout on your device; nothing is uploaded.

The free sampler sign-up

The free printable sampler sign-up is handled by Payhip, the platform we use to deliver it. When you sign up, you give your email address to Payhip on their site, and their privacy policy applies. We use that address only to send the sampler and occasional %%BRAND%% emails, and you can unsubscribe at any time from any email.

Links to our products

If you follow a link to buy one of our products, the store that sells it handles your purchase under its own privacy policy.

Cookies

This site sets no tracking cookies and no advertising cookies. The calculator's saved list uses your browser's local storage, which stays on your device.

Hosting

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep basic technical logs, such as IP addresses, to run and secure the service. We do not receive or use those logs.
